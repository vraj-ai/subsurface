# Quality Grades are receipt-backed and use the weakest measured dimension

Subsurface grades a Site or prepared candidate from `A+` through `F`, or
`Incomplete`, and the overall grade is the lowest measured quality dimension.
It owns the strict normalization while consuming existing project tooling where
available; a model may provide a cited provisional critique for missing coverage
but cannot invent a metric, remove `Incomplete`, or make a candidate eligible
for automatic publication. This deliberately trades flattering aggregate scores
for a grade that cannot hide a serious weakness behind stronger dimensions.
